Who we are

Your condo can do more than sit empty, or rent at the same rate as every unit around it.

Cozy Stay helps unit owners in Cebu and Davao turn their condominium into a well-styled, well-run short-term rental. We handle the listing, the guests, the cleaning and the numbers, using a property management system and apps we built ourselves. You own the unit. We make it perform.

We are a small, hands-on company. Our founder handles guest relations, listings and owner reporting personally, supported by cleaning staff in each city who work from our Cleaner App.

Our promise. We do not promise a fixed income, because results depend on the unit, the building and the season. We promise that your unit is presented well, priced thoughtfully and managed carefully every day, with records you can check yourself.

    How we manage your property

    Ten stages, the same for every unit

    Your part. You set your goals and owner-use dates, approve the fee, cleaning rates, listing and any purchases, agree on a minimum rate, and approve major repairs. We handle everything else.
    Property portfolio

    The units we manage today

    Each unit is privately owned. Our founder personally styled the four Sanremo Oasis units: same building, same rates, four distinct stays that attract different guests.

    Building amenities such as pools and gyms are run by each building's administration, not by Cozy Stay.

    Services and fees

    What we handle, and how we charge
