Who we are

More than a listing, less work for you.

Your condo can do more than sit empty, or rent at the same rate as every unit around it.

Cozy Stay helps owners in Cebu and Davao turn their condominium into a well-styled, well-run short-term rental. We handle the listing, the guests, the cleaning and the numbers, using a management system and apps we built ourselves. You own the unit. We make it perform.

We are a small, hands-on company. Our founder handles guest relations, listings and owner reporting personally, supported by cleaning staff in each city who work from our Cleaner App.

Our promise

We don't promise a fixed income, because results depend on the unit, the building and the season. We promise that your unit is presented well, priced thoughtfully and cared for every day, with records you can check yourself.
